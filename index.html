04 · Reading the evidence

Keep the stages separate

The page surfaces candidate relationships for review and preserves where the evidence stops.

Plan vs appropriation

PIP “Investment Targets – 2026” are planned investment targets, not enacted appropriations or realized expenditure.

Candidate vs verified link

BetterGov returns broader P/A/P families. This pilot found no one-to-one PIP project crosswalk to a GAA line.

GAA vs execution

An appropriation does not establish allotment release, obligation, disbursement, procurement, delivery, or outcome.

Chapter 4 objectives used

Distilled from the local core-objective extraction.

Employability

Align education and TVET with labor-market needs; promote lifelong learning and recognition of prior learning.

Employment access

Strengthen employment facilitation, labor-market information, and targeted support, especially for youth and disadvantaged groups.

Shared governance

Coordinate government, employers, workers, and education providers, with equity, gender, and green competencies in view.
