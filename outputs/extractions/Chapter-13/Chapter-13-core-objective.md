## Chapter 13: Ensure Peace and Security and Enhance Administration of Justice - Core objectives

The chapter aims to sustain peace and security while improving the fairness, efficiency, and accessibility of the justice system.

- Support peace agreements and transitions, protect conflict-affected communities, and address drivers of conflict through coordinated, peace-sensitive development.
- Safeguard people from criminality, natural hazards, and other security threats; protect territorial integrity and strengthen security institutions.
- Improve case disposition, coordination, evidence management, digital systems, and access to affordable legal services.
- Improve conditions and rehabilitation in corrections and support productive reintegration of persons deprived of liberty.
- Apply cross-cutting safeguards for human rights, rule of law, gender, vulnerable groups, and social cohesion.
