## Chapter 02: Promote Human and Social Development - Core objectives

The chapter aims to improve human and social development through better health, education and lifelong learning, and more livable communities.

- **Health:** Improve the social conditions that shape health, enable healthy choices, widen access to quality and efficient care, and strengthen health systems.
- **Education and lifelong learning:** Improve inclusive, adaptive basic education; strengthen TVET and higher education, research, and workforce relevance; and improve coordination and governance across the education system.
- **Livable communities:** Strengthen social cohesion, environmental quality, housing and the built environment, essential utilities and mobility, and responsive community governance.
