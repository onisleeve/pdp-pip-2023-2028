## Chapter 06: Revitalize Industry - Core objectives

The chapter aims to revitalize industry by expanding domestic production, moving firms into higher-value activities, and strengthening links across sectors and regions.

- Broaden domestic production and supplier capacity through business matching, regional industrialization, targeted support, shared facilities, and research and technology adoption.
- Improve industry competitiveness and value addition by addressing supply-chain, logistics, energy, digitalization, workforce, quality, and standards constraints.
- Develop a more dynamic industrial ecosystem, including science-based industries, technology adoption, entrepreneurship, and downstream activities.
- Strengthen connections among industry, services, trade, investment, research, education, and regional clusters.
