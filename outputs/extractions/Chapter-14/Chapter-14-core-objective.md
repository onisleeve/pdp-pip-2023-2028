## Chapter 14: Practice Good Governance and Improve Bureaucratic Efficiency - Core objectives

The chapter aims to improve governance and bureaucratic performance through deeper public participation, stronger accountability, more capable institutions, and a more effective public service.

- Make participation and access to public information more meaningful, inclusive, and accessible.
- Strengthen public feedback, transparency in public spending, and implementation of integrity and anti-corruption measures.
- Rationalize government functions and improve public systems, digital services, and agency productivity.
- Support a competent, motivated, agile, and resilient public workforce.
