## Chapter 05: Modernize Agriculture and Agribusiness - Core objectives

The chapter aims to modernize agriculture and agribusiness by raising production efficiency, expanding market opportunities, strengthening value chains, and improving sector institutions.

- Improve farm and non-farm productivity and incomes through consolidation, technology, and better access to production inputs.
- Expand markets and value-adding opportunities for agriculture, forestry, and fisheries (AFF) products, supported by physical and digital infrastructure and a stronger investment environment.
- Make AFF value chains more resilient to climate, disasters, supply disruptions, and price shocks.
- Strengthen agricultural education and job-skills matching.
