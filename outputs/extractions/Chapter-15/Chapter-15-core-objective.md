## Chapter 15: Accelerate Climate Action and Strengthen Disaster Resilience - Core objectives

The chapter aims to accelerate climate action and strengthen resilience to climate and disaster risks across communities, ecosystems, institutions, and the economy.

- Improve the capacity of local governments and communities to prevent, prepare for, and respond to disasters and climate risks.
- Strengthen partnerships, knowledge, and investment aligned with local adaptation and risk-reduction priorities.
- Enhance ecosystem resilience and support nature-based industries and enterprises.
- Enable a low-carbon transition through national climate commitments, private investment, and wider markets for low-carbon technologies.
- Improve climate and risk information, natural-capital accounting, governance, and sustainable finance.
