## Chapter 08: Advance Research and Development, Technology, and Innovation - Core objectives

The chapter aims to advance research, development, technology, and innovation (STI), connecting knowledge creation to practical adoption and inclusive economic value.

- Strengthen basic research, education, and national investment in research and development.
- Make research more responsive to markets and users through public-private collaboration and stronger research partnerships.
- Expand technology transfer, extension, adoption, intellectual-property support, and commercialization of publicly funded research.
- Accelerate innovation and entrepreneurship through skilled workers, mentoring, innovation hubs, and better access to finance.
