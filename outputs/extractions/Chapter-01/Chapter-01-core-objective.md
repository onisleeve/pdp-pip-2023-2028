## Chapter 01: A Plan for Economic and Social Transformation - Core objectives

The chapter sets the PDP’s overall direction: accelerate economic and social transformation toward a prosperous, inclusive, and resilient society, consistent with AmBisyon Natin 2040.

- Sustain broad-based economic growth while transforming production sectors to create more and better-quality jobs and strengthen enterprise competitiveness.
- Reduce poverty and vulnerability, protect purchasing power, and rebuild human development affected by the pandemic.
- Develop people’s capabilities through health, education, lifelong learning, and social protection; strengthen resilience in communities and institutions.
- Maintain macroeconomic and fiscal stability and coordinate implementation across government and society.

This is a summary of the chapter’s headline direction and targets, not a transcription of its indicator table.
