## Chapter 03: Reduce Vulnerabilities and Protect Purchasing Power - Core objectives

The chapter seeks to reduce vulnerabilities and protect purchasing power by improving food security, nutrition, and social protection.

- Ensure a sufficient and stable supply of food, and expand consumers’ access to affordable, safe, and nutritious food.
- Improve nutrition across all ages.
- Build a universal, modern, and integrated social protection system that mitigates lifecycle and economic risks and can respond to disasters and other shocks.
- Improve social protection governance and address risks affecting vulnerable groups and communities.
