## Chapter 07: Reinvigorate Services - Core objectives

The chapter aims to reinvigorate services by expanding markets, strengthening the value offered through creativity and innovation, and improving connections across industries.

- Improve market reach and service-sector growth, including tourism, creative industries, e-commerce, and business services.
- Develop a competitive, creative, and skilled workforce and improve access to research, development, startup finance, and intellectual-property support.
- Use digital infrastructure and services to improve transport, logistics, connectivity, and business support.
- Strengthen service-sector data and collaboration among government, industry, and education institutions.
