## Chapter 16: Plan Implementation, Monitoring, and Evaluation - Core objectives

The chapter sets out how the PDP should be implemented, monitored, and evaluated so agencies can coordinate delivery, track results, and adjust policy based on evidence.

- Align agency plans, policies, budgets, and programs with the PDP and coordinate implementation through designated inter-agency bodies.
- Monitor outcomes through the Results Matrix, regular reporting, and stronger data collection, analysis, and public reporting.
- Build government evaluation capacity and conduct priority evaluations to inform implementation and future plans.
- Use evaluation findings and public communication to strengthen learning, transparency, accountability, and evidence-based decisions.
