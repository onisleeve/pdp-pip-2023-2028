## Chapter 09: Promote Trade and Investments - Core objectives

The chapter aims to strengthen the Philippines’ position in trade and investment by improving export competitiveness and attracting investments aligned with national development priorities.

- Remove constraints to export growth, diversify exports, strengthen value-chain linkages, and pursue forward-looking trade agreements.
- Improve the business and quality-infrastructure conditions that help firms compete in global markets.
- Increase and target investments toward trade, skills upgrading, rural and location-specific opportunities, and sustainability.
- Coordinate trade and investment promotion across government.
