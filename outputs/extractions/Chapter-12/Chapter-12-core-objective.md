## Chapter 12: Expand and Upgrade Infrastructure - Core objectives

The chapter aims to expand and upgrade infrastructure so it is better planned, connected, resilient, and able to support economic and social development.

- Improve infrastructure planning, programming, asset management, resilience, and financing.
- Provide inclusive local and international connectivity through transport and related infrastructure.
- Improve water security and the ecological integrity and resilience of water systems.
- Provide affordable, accessible, reliable, and cleaner energy.
- Strengthen social infrastructure, including infrastructure that supports health, education, and environmental services.
