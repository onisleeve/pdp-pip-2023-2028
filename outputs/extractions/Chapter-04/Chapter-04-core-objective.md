## Chapter 04: Increase Income-earning Ability - Core objectives

The chapter aims to increase Filipinos’ ability to earn income by developing relevant skills, expanding access to employment, and improving shared labor-market governance.

- Align basic, technical-vocational, and higher education with changing labor-market needs; promote lifelong learning and recognize prior learning.
- Expand employment facilitation, labor-market information, and targeted employment support, especially for youth and groups facing disadvantage.
- Strengthen coordination among government, employers, workers, and education providers, with attention to equitable access, gender, and green competencies.
