## Chapter 10: Promote Competition and Improve Regulatory Efficiency - Core objectives

The chapter aims to strengthen competition by reducing anti-competitive practices, lowering barriers to market entry, and making regulation more efficient.

- Improve the identification and enforcement response to competition issues, including through research and compliance programs.
- Implement economic liberalization reforms and enable fair access to essential facilities and infrastructure.
- Reduce unnecessary or overlapping regulation and strengthen regulatory management and ease-of-doing-business systems.
- Apply a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to competition, including in digital markets.
