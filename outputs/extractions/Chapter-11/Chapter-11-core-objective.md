## Chapter 11: Ensure Macroeconomic Stability and Expand Inclusive and Innovative Finance - Core objectives

The chapter aims to support macroeconomic stability and inclusive transformation through a healthy, innovative financial sector and sound fiscal management.

- Broaden financial inclusion, encourage responsible financial innovation, protect consumers, and maintain financial-sector stability.
- Establish a simpler and more equitable tax system, improve revenue collection and tax and customs administration, and mobilize other revenue sources.
- Improve budget and public financial management, strengthen local-government finance, and manage public debt sustainably.
- Use data, digital systems, and coordination to improve the effectiveness of financial and fiscal institutions.
